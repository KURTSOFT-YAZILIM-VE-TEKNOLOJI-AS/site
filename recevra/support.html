Common questions

Are my receipts uploaded?

No. Receipt images, recognized text and expenses stay on your phone, and recognition runs on the device. See the privacy policy for what analytics and ads do receive.

Why was my total not read correctly?

Faded, crumpled or low-light receipts are harder to read. Retake the photo or correct the value on the review screen. Recevra leaves a field empty rather than guessing, and nothing is saved until you confirm it.

I declined camera access. Can I still use the app?

Yes. You can import photos and files or add expenses by hand. To scan with the camera later, allow it in your device settings.

How many free scans do I get?

The free version includes 3 receipt scans per install, counted when you save a scanned expense. Manual entry is unlimited, and your existing records are never locked.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

Restore a purchase from Settings or the subscription screen. Cancel in the subscription settings of Google Play or the App Store; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. Where consent applies, go to Settings > Privacy options. Recevra Premium removes ads.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Full steps are on the data deletion page.

Still stuck?

Email support@kurtsoft.com.tr and describe your device, OS version, and what happened. Please do not attach receipts that show personal or payment details.
