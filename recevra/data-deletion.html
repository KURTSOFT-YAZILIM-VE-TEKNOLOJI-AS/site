Data Deletion

1. What data exists

  • On your device: receipt images, recognized text, expenses, budgets, recurring expenses, categories and your settings. None of this is sent to us.
  • With service providers: app activity, crash reports and identifiers (Google Firebase), ad data (Google AdMob, free version), and purchase information (Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat), as described in the Privacy Policy.

Recevra has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

2. How to delete your data

  • Inside the app: Settings > Delete all data permanently removes every expense, receipt image, recurring schedule and budget on the phone. You can also delete a single expense, or remove receipt images under Settings > Receipt images.
  • Clear app data (Android): Settings > Apps > Recevra > Storage > Clear storage. This removes everything stored on your device immediately.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling Recevra removes the app and all of its local data. Reinstalling creates new random identifiers that are not linked to the old ones. There is no cloud copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's settings.
  • Data held by service providers: email support@kurtsoft.com.tr with the subject "Data deletion". Because the app has no accounts, include any detail that helps us find your data. We answer within 30 days.

3. Data kept after deletion

Delete all data keeps your settings and the app's random support ID on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase after its retention period. Purchase records are kept by the store and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal and tax obligations. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in the store's subscription settings.
