Receipt scanner and expense tracker

Scan the receipt. Understand the spending.

Recevra turns paper and digital receipts into a clear picture of where your money goes. Text recognition runs on your phone, and your receipts and expenses stay there.

01 / Inside

From a crumpled receipt to a clear month.

Capture, check and understand your spending without creating an account.

Scan

Camera, photos or files

Photograph a paper receipt, or import a photo or PDF. If you decline the camera, photos, files and manual entry keep working.

Review

You confirm every expense

Recognition suggests the merchant, date, total and category. Nothing is saved until you check it, and uncertain fields are left for you to fill in.

Track

Manual entry and search

Add cash, online orders or rent by hand. Find any expense by merchant, note or category, and edit, delete or undo.

Insights

See where it goes

Monthly totals, spending by category, trends and a weekly summary, all recalculated from your own records. Each currency keeps its own total.

Plan

Budgets and recurring costs

Set a monthly budget, watch what is left, and track recurring expenses such as subscriptions and rent.

Languages

Seven languages

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Turkish, with amounts and dates shown the way your region writes them.

02 / Privacy

Your receipts stay on your phone.

Receipt images, recognized text and expenses are stored on your device and are not sent to us. The free version shows ads, and the app uses analytics and crash reporting, which never receive receipt contents, amounts or merchant names.
