Privacy Policy

1. Summary

Recevra is a receipt scanner and expense tracker. Your receipt images, recognized text and expenses are stored in the app's private storage and processed on your phone; they are not sent to us, and no account is required. The app does send technical data to service providers for analytics, crash reporting, ads and subscriptions, as described below. That data never includes receipt contents, amounts or merchant names.

2. Data we collect

Stored only on your device, not collected by us: receipt images, recognized text, expenses, budgets, recurring expenses, categories and your settings.

Sent to service providers:

  • App activity: screens opened, features used, whether an operation succeeded or failed, and how long it took.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: error traces, device model, operating system version, app version and start-up times.
  • Device and app identifiers: a random support ID created by the app, the Firebase app-instance and installation IDs, the advertising ID, and an anonymous RevenueCat ID.
  • Purchase information: your subscription status and purchase history. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Ad data (free version): the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

3. How we use it

  • App activity, diagnostics and crash logs: to understand which features are used and to find and fix problems.
  • Identifiers: to group analytics and crash reports by installation, and to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase information: to unlock Recevra Premium and restore your purchase.
  • Ad data: to show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud.

4. Third parties and SDKs

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): app activity, crash reports, identifiers and app settings.
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: ad requests and your consent choices (free version only).
  • Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat: subscription purchases and status.
  • Google ML Kit: text recognition, which runs on your device. Receipt images and recognized text are not sent to us.

Provider policies: Google (how Google uses data from apps), RevenueCat. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and purchases

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice before ads are requested; you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy options. Analytics and crash reporting are not covered by this ad consent choice. Recevra Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes ads, lifts scan limits and unlocks deeper insights.

7. Children

Recevra is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Receipts, expenses and settings stay on your device until you delete them; uninstalling the app removes them. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for a limited time under its retention settings and then deleted automatically. Purchases are kept by the store and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal obligations. See the data deletion page for how to remove your data.

9. Security

Receipts and expenses stay in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection of information sent over the internet.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
