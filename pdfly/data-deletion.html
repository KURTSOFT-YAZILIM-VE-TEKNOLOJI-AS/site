Data Deletion

1. What data exists

  • On your device: your documents, recognized text, signatures, saved PDF passwords, AI history, the downloaded AI model and your settings. None of this is sent to us.
  • With service providers: app activity, crash reports and identifiers (Google Firebase), ad data (Google AdMob, free version), and purchase information (Google Play and RevenueCat), as described in the Privacy Policy.

PDFly has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

2. How to delete your data

  • Inside the app: delete files from your library, and use Settings > Clear AI history to remove chats, summaries and notes.
  • Clear app data: Settings > Apps > PDFly > Storage > Clear storage. This removes everything stored on your device immediately.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling PDFly removes the app and all of its local data. Reinstalling creates new random identifiers that are not linked to the old ones.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's Google settings.
  • Data held by service providers: email support@kurtsoft.com.tr with the subject "Data deletion". Because the app has no accounts, include any detail that helps us find your data. We answer within 30 days.

3. Data kept after deletion

Analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase after its retention period. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal and tax obligations. Nothing else is kept.
