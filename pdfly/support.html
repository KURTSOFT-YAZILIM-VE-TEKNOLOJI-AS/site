Common questions

Does PDFly upload my documents?

No. Scanning, text recognition, conversions, translation and the AI assistant all run on your phone, and your files stay in the app's private storage. A file leaves the app only when you share, print or save it somewhere yourself.

Why does the AI assistant need a download?

The AI model runs on your phone, so it has to be downloaded once: about 1 to 2.6 GB depending on your phone. Use Wi-Fi for the download. After that, the assistant works without an internet connection. The AI assistant is part of PDFly Pro.

The AI says my phone isn't supported.

The on-device model needs enough memory (RAM) to run. PDFly picks a smaller model on phones with less memory, but very low-memory phones cannot run either one. All other tools work normally on these phones.

How do I restore or cancel PDFly Pro?

To restore, open the PDFly Pro screen and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google account. To cancel, open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or tap Manage subscription in PDFly's settings.

Where are my files, and how do I back them up?

Your library lives inside the app. Use Save to device to copy a file to your phone's storage, or Share to send it to another app or a cloud drive. Uninstalling PDFly deletes the files stored inside it, so back up anything you want to keep first.

I forgot the password of a locked PDF.

PDF passwords are real encryption, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered by you or by us. If you turned on biometric unlock for saved passwords, you may still be able to open the file with your fingerprint or screen lock on the same phone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete files in the app, clear its storage or uninstall it to remove everything on your device. For analytics and crash data, see the data deletion page.

Still stuck?

Email support@kurtsoft.com.tr and describe your device, Android version, and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
