Privacy Policy

1. Summary

PDFly is a document scanner, PDF reader and editor with an on-device AI assistant. Your documents are stored in the app's private storage and processed on your phone, including text recognition (OCR), conversions, signatures, saved PDF passwords, translation, read aloud and the AI assistant. We do not receive your documents, the text in them, or your questions and answers to the AI assistant. A file leaves the app only when you share, print, export or save it somewhere yourself. The app has no user accounts. To keep it working, it collects app activity, crash reports, identifiers and purchase information through the services listed below.

2. Data we collect

  • App activity: screens opened, buttons and tools used, whether an operation succeeded or failed, how long it took, and rough counts such as the number of pages. These are fixed labels and numbers, never document content.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: error traces, device model, Android version, app version and start-up times.
  • Device and app identifiers: a random support ID created by the app, the Firebase app-instance and installation IDs, the Android advertising ID, and an anonymous RevenueCat user ID.
  • Purchase information: your subscription status and purchase history. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Ad data (free version): the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

3. How we use it

  • App activity, diagnostics and crash logs: to understand which features are used and to find and fix problems.
  • Identifiers: to group analytics and crash reports by installation, and to show and measure ads.
  • Purchase information: to unlock PDFly Pro and restore your purchase.
  • Ad data: to show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud.

4. Third parties and SDKs

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): app activity, crash reports, identifiers and app settings.
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: ad requests and your consent choices (free version only).
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: subscription purchases and status.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): the one-time download of the AI model file. Like any download, the server sees your IP address. No document data is sent.
  • Google ML Kit: one-time downloads of translation language files. Text recognition and translation then run on your device.

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. Depending on the engine and voice chosen in your system settings, that engine may process the spoken text under its own privacy policy.

Provider policies: Google (how Google uses data from apps), ML Kit, RevenueCat, Hugging Face. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising and purchases

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice before showing personalized ads; you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy options. Analytics and crash reporting are not covered by this ad consent choice. PDFly Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes ads, unlocks the AI assistant and private documents, and lifts tool limits.

7. Children

PDFly is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Documents, signatures, saved passwords and AI history stay on your device until you delete them; uninstalling the app removes them, along with the downloaded AI model and settings. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for a limited time under its retention settings and then deleted automatically. Purchases are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal obligations. See the data deletion page for how to remove your data.

9. Security

Content you create stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Saved PDF passwords are sealed with a device-bound Android Keystore key. Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection of information sent over the internet.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
