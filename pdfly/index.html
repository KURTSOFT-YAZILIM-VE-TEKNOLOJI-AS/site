01 / Inside

Everything a PDF needs, without leaving the app.

Scanning, text recognition, conversions and the AI assistant all run on your phone, so your files are processed where they already are.

Scan

Document scanner

Modes for documents, receipts, ID cards, books and whiteboards. Edge detection with auto capture, batch scanning, crop and cleanup filters.

OCR

Text recognition

Extract text from photos and PDF pages in Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Devanagari scripts, and make scanned PDFs searchable.

Read

Reader and read aloud

Search, thumbnails, night mode and vertical or horizontal paging. Listen to a PDF with text-to-speech and follow the highlighted sentence.

Edit

Annotate and sign

Highlight, underline, strike through and add notes. Draw or type a signature and place it on any page. Add text watermarks or erase content.

Convert

Convert and organize

PDF to and from Word, Excel and PowerPoint, images to PDF or Word. Merge, split, extract, reorder and compress, with folders and favorites.

AI · Pro

AI assistant on your device

Chat with a PDF and jump to the cited page, or get summaries, key points and study notes. The model downloads once; documents are not uploaded.

02 / Privacy

Your documents never leave your phone.

Files, recognized text, signatures, saved passwords and AI conversations stay in the app's private storage. PDFly has no accounts. It uses Google services for ads, analytics and crash reports, and RevenueCat for subscriptions, which receive identifiers and usage data but never your documents.
