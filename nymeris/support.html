Common questions

Are my dreams uploaded?

No. Dreams, moods and interpretations stay on your phone, and the AI runs on the device. See the privacy policy for what analytics, ads and downloads do receive.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Writing, reading, searching and editing dreams works without internet. The AI interpreter also works offline once its model and the language packs it needs are downloaded. Downloads, purchases and restoring a purchase need a connection.

Why is the AI interpreter not available on my phone?

The on-device model needs a 64-bit ARM device with enough memory and free storage. On phones that do not qualify, the journal keeps working without AI. If the download fails, check your connection and free space, then tap Try again.

How large is the AI model download?

About 2.6 GB on most phones and about 1 GB on phones with less memory, plus extra free space while it installs. The app shows the size before you start. Consider using Wi-Fi.

My dream's language is not supported. What can I do?

Interpretations are available for English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi and Afrikaans. Dreams in other languages stay in your journal; they cannot be interpreted yet.

What happens to my interpretations if Pro ends?

They stay. Every dream and every saved interpretation remains readable. Only starting new interpretations needs Pro.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Restore a purchase from Profile > Restore purchases or from the Pro screen. Cancel in the subscription settings of Google Play or the App Store; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I change my ad consent choice?

Yes. Where consent applies, go to Profile > Ad privacy choices. NYMERIS Pro removes ads.

How do I delete my data?

Use Profile > Delete all dream records, or uninstall the app. Full steps are on the data deletion page.

Still stuck?

Email support@kurtsoft.com.tr and describe your device, OS version, and what happened. Please do not include the text of your dreams.
