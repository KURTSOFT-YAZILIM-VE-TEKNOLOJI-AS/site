Privacy Policy

1. Summary

NYMERIS is a dream journal with an optional on-device AI interpreter. Your dreams, moods and interpretations are stored in the app's private storage on your phone; they are not sent to us, and no account is required. AI interpretations and the translations they need are generated on your phone, not on a server. The app does send technical data to service providers for analytics, crash reporting, ads, subscriptions and downloads, as described below. That data never includes dream titles, dream text, moods, dates or interpretations.

2. Data we collect

Stored only on your device, not collected by us: dream titles and text, dream dates, icons, waking moods, AI interpretations, and your settings such as the app language.

Sent to service providers:

  • App activity: screens opened, features used, whether an operation succeeded or failed, and how long it took.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: error traces, device model, operating system version, app version, start-up times, the last screen opened and whether Pro and ads are active. Error messages are reduced to their technical type so that they cannot carry dream text.
  • Device and app identifiers: the Firebase app-instance and installation IDs, the advertising ID, an anonymous RevenueCat ID and a Google ML Kit installation ID.
  • Purchase information: your subscription status and purchase history. Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Ad data (free version): the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads.
  • Download requests (Pro): when you download the AI model or a language pack, the download server receives a standard request with your IP address. No dream content is included.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

3. How we use it

  • App activity, diagnostics and crash logs: to understand which features are used and to find and fix problems.
  • Identifiers: to group analytics and crash reports by installation, to show and measure ads, and to operate on-device translation.
  • Purchase information: to unlock NYMERIS Pro and restore your purchase.
  • Ad data: to show and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud.
  • Download requests: to deliver the AI model and language files to your phone.

4. On-device AI

The AI interpreter is optional and available with NYMERIS Pro. It needs a one-time download of an AI model (about 1 to 2.6 GB, depending on your device) from Hugging Face, and of Google ML Kit language packs when a dream or interpretation is not in English. Downloads need an internet connection. After that, interpreting a dream works without internet: the dream is translated to English on your phone, interpreted on your phone, and the result is translated back on your phone. Neither the dream nor the interpretation leaves the device.

The model and language files are stored in the app's private storage and can be removed from the local AI model screen with Remove model.

5. Third parties and SDKs

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): app activity, crash reports, identifiers and app settings.
  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform: ad requests and your consent choices (free version only).
  • Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat: subscription purchases and status.
  • Google ML Kit (language identification and translation): runs on your device. Language packs are downloaded from Google, and the SDK creates an installation ID and fetches its own configuration from Google. Dream text and interpretations are not sent.
  • Hugging Face: hosts the AI model files that Pro users download. It receives the download request only.

Provider policies: Google (how Google uses data from apps), RevenueCat, Hugging Face. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Advertising and purchases

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are not shown while you write, read or edit a dream, or on AI interpretation screens. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice before ads are requested; you can change it at any time in Profile > Ad privacy choices. Analytics and crash reporting are not covered by this ad consent choice. NYMERIS Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes ads and unlocks the on-device AI interpreter.

8. Children

NYMERIS is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Dreams, interpretations and settings stay on your device until you delete them; uninstalling the app removes them. If device backup is turned on, your operating system may include the journal in your Google or iCloud backup under your backup settings; we have no access to those backups. AI model files are never included in backups. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for a limited time under its retention settings and then deleted automatically. Purchases are kept by the store and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal obligations. See the data deletion page for how to remove your data.

10. Security

Dreams and interpretations stay in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. They are not encrypted by the app beyond the protection your device provides. Network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS), and downloaded AI model files are checked against a known checksum before use. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection of information sent over the internet.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
