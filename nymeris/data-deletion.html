Data Deletion

1. What data exists

  • On your device: dreams, moods, AI interpretations and your settings, plus the AI model and language packs if you downloaded them. None of this is sent to us.
  • With service providers: app activity, crash reports and identifiers (Google Firebase), ad data (Google AdMob, free version), purchase information (Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat), and the ML Kit installation ID (Google), as described in the Privacy Policy.

NYMERIS has no accounts, so there is no account to delete.

2. How to delete your data

  • Inside the app: Profile > Delete all dream records permanently removes every dream and every interpretation on the phone. You can also delete a single dream from its detail screen, which removes its interpretations too.
  • AI model and language packs: open the local AI model screen from Profile > AI model status and tap Remove model. This deletes the model, its caches and the language packs.
  • Clear app data (Android): Settings > Apps > NYMERIS > Storage > Clear storage. This removes everything stored on your device immediately.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling NYMERIS removes the app and all of its local data. Reinstalling creates new random identifiers that are not linked to the old ones. We keep no cloud copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered by us.
  • Device backups: if your operating system backed up the app's journal to Google or iCloud, manage or delete that backup in your device's backup settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's settings.
  • Data held by service providers: email support@kurtsoft.com.tr with the subject "Data deletion". Because the app has no accounts, include any detail that helps us find your data. We answer within 30 days.

3. Data kept after deletion

Delete all dream records keeps your settings, such as the app language, and any downloaded AI model on your device until you remove the model, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Google ML Kit may keep a small cache per language after the language packs are removed; clearing the app's data removes it. Analytics and crash data are deleted automatically by Firebase after its retention period. Purchase records are kept by the store and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal and tax obligations. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in the store's subscription settings.
