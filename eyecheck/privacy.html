Privacy Policy for Eye Test & Eye Exercises

This policy explains what Eye Test & Eye Exercises (package com.kurtsoft.eyecheck) does with your information. It applies to the Android app distributed through Google Play.

Information the app collects

The app has no account and no sign-in. Everything you measure is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your device.

Data sent off your device

Your screening results never leave your device. There is no cloud sync, no server-side account, and no upload of measurements, camera frames, or reports.

The following services use the network:

  • Usage events and crash reports, controlled separately by the analytics and crash-reporting switches in Settings under Privacy.
  • Subscription checks. When you open the subscription screen, buy Pro, or restore a purchase, the app talks to Google Play and RevenueCat to find out what you are entitled to.
  • Update checks. When the app comes to the foreground it asks Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version has been published. The request carries an installation identifier and general device and app information; it sends nothing you entered and no measurement.
  • Ads in the free version. Google AdMob requests and shows ads and receives the advertising ID, IP address, general device information and ad interactions. It never receives a screening result, a profile name or a birth year. With Pro no ad is requested.
  • Ad privacy choices. Google User Messaging Platform contacts Google to obtain consent requirements and present any required form. Ad availability follows the consent state returned by that service.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, analytics and crash reporting start off. A one-time screen during setup asks whether you want to turn them on, and declining is offered exactly as prominently as accepting. Declining sends no event at all. Elsewhere they start on and can be switched off at any time in Settings under Privacy.

Ad consent is a separate choice. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks before any ad is requested, and you can review or withdraw that choice at any time in Settings under Privacy, "Ad privacy choices". If consent is not given, the app follows Google's returned consent state and requests ads only when that state permits it. The form and available choices depend on region and Google's configured messages.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad when the app opens or returns from the background, a banner above the bottom navigation, a medium rectangle on the Measure, Plan and Exercise tabs, and occasionally a full-screen ad after an exercise session or when you leave a result screen. Ads never appear during a measurement, on a result, invalid-measurement, report or clinician screen, on the paywall or during setup. Ad sound is muted and ad content is limited to Google's "Teen" rating or lower.

Pro is a monthly or annual subscription. It adds six more measurements, your full history with charts, the PDF report, backup and transfer to a new device, the longer exercise routines, and removes all ads. Trial availability and duration depend on eligibility and the offer shown by Google Play; the four core tests and the free exercises stay free. With Pro no ad is requested at all. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

Data retention and deletion

Measurements, profiles, exercise history, plans and reports stay on your device until you remove them. Settings contains a Delete all data action that clears the active profile's screening results and exercise sessions, clears plans and saved PDF reports, and disables reminders. Screening results and exercise sessions belonging to other profiles remain. This action does not remove profile names or every setting. Uninstalling removes app-private local data; any reports or backup files you previously exported must be deleted separately from their destination.

Because none of that is on a server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no server-side copy of your screening history to export. Analytics and crash data held by Google are subject to the applicable Firebase retention settings and policies. You can stop future analytics and crash collection using their separate switches in Settings; this does not itself erase records already stored by a service. Remote Config update checks are separate from these switches. Purchase records are held by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as they need them to service the subscription. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's own retention policies. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID changes its availability for future use; it does not itself request deletion of data already held by Google.

For local deletion steps, exported files and service-held data, see Data Deletion.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience health and fitness app and is not directed at children under 13.

It does include a child profile so that a parent can run a screening for their child. That profile stores only the name and, optionally, the birth year typed by the parent, and it stays on the device with everything else. Child profile names, birth years and screening results are not sent to our services. The network services described above may still receive device identifiers and technical data; using a child profile does not disable advertising or change the ad consent configuration. A child profile should be set up and used with a parent or guardian present. Ads in the free version are the same whichever profile is active and are limited to Google's "Teen" content rating; nothing about the child profile is passed to the ad network.

Security

Content you create stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Network requests use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection of information transmitted over the internet.
