Data Deletion

1. What data exists

The app has no account or sign-in. Screening results, profiles, exercise sessions, plans and saved reports are held in app-private storage on your device. The app does not upload screening results or camera frames. Reports you share and backup files you export remain wherever you send or save them. Some settings may also be included in Android's device backup if you enable it.

Network services can hold technical data: Firebase Analytics receives usage events and an app-instance identifier when analytics is enabled; Firebase Crashlytics receives diagnostics when crash reporting is enabled. Remote Config update checks use an installation identifier separately from those switches. AdMob can receive advertising IDs, IP addresses, device information and ad interactions in the free version. Google Play and RevenueCat hold purchase and subscription records when those services are used. These services do not receive screening results, profile names or birth years. See the Privacy Policy for details.

2. Delete data on your device

  • Inside the app: Settings → Delete all data clears the active profile's screening results and exercise sessions, clears plans and saved PDF reports, and disables reminders. It does not remove profile names or reset every setting. Screening results and exercise sessions for other profiles remain; switch profiles and repeat if needed.
  • Remove all app-private local data: in Android, open Settings → Apps → Eye Test & Eye Exercises → Storage → Clear storage. Uninstalling also removes app-private data from the device. Android may restore backed-up settings on reinstall; manage your device backup separately.
  • Exported files: delete shared reports and exported backup files from the locations where you saved or sent them, including any cloud storage or recipient copies you control. Clearing app storage does not delete those copies.

3. Stop collection and request help with service-held data

  • Analytics and crash reporting: use their separate switches in Settings → Privacy to stop future collection. Both start off in the EEA and the UK until enabled. These switches do not erase records already held by Firebase or stop Remote Config update checks.
  • Advertising: review available consent options in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Google settings. This changes its availability for future use; it does not itself delete records already held by Google. With Pro, no ad is requested.
  • Subscriptions: uninstalling or deleting local data does not cancel a subscription or erase purchase records. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

To ask about data held by a service, email support@kurtsoft.com.tr and describe the request. Do not send screening results, payment card details or passwords. We can explain the available process and whether records can be identified for the request. Service retention rules and any billing or legal retention requirements still apply. Local deletion does not send a deletion request to Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play or RevenueCat.
