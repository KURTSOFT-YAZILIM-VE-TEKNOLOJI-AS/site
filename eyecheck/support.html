Common questions

Can this replace an eye exam?

No. Eye Test & Eye Exercises is a screening tool. It tells you whether something looks worth checking, in three categories: no finding, mild, or marked. It never produces a prescription and it cannot detect everything an eye examination can. If a result concerns you, or if you notice sudden changes, see an eye care professional.

Why does it need the camera?

The front camera estimates viewing distance from iris landmarks and supports blink counting. Accuracy depends on your device, lighting and test setup. Frames are processed in memory on the device; they are not saved or uploaded. Some tests require camera access; exercises remain available without it.

The app says my measurement is invalid. What now?

That is deliberate: when a measurement cannot be trusted, the app refuses to produce a result instead of guessing. The usual causes are being outside the 15–80 cm range, moving during the test, poor lighting, or a screen whose reported size is out of the expected range. The invalid screen names the reason; fix that and run the test again.

Why doesn’t my quick scan show up in the chart?

Only detailed measurements feed the trend, the change analysis and the report. A quick scan has too few repetitions to separate a real change from noise, so including it would make random variation look like a trend. Run the detailed measurement about every three months.

My reminders arrive late.

Battery saving can hold a reminder back by around a minute, which is normal. Some manufacturers — Xiaomi and Huawei among them — block background start entirely for apps that are not whitelisted; check your device’s battery and autostart settings for Eye Test & Eye Exercises.

What do I get with Pro, and how do I cancel?

Pro unlocks colour, contrast, Amsler, mirror mode and the two near-point checks; full history, charts, change analysis, PDF reports, manual backup and restore; and the 5- and 8-minute routines. It also removes ads. The four core checks and free exercises stay free. Monthly and annual subscriptions are offered through Google Play. Any trial depends on eligibility and the offer shown before purchase. Manage or cancel in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. Use “Restore purchases” with the Google Play account used for the purchase to restore an eligible subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Settings contains Delete all data. It clears the active profile's screening results and exercise sessions, clears plans and saved PDF reports, and disables reminders. It leaves profile names, some settings and other profiles' results and sessions. For complete local removal, exported files and service-held records, follow the data deletion page.
